FAQs

Common questions about SEO services in Kenya.

How long does SEO take?

SEO usually takes time because search engines need to recrawl, compare, and reassess pages after meaningful improvements are published.

Can SEO help a new website?

Yes. Strong structure, useful service pages, and clean technical setup give a new site a far better starting point.

Do I need separate pages for each service?

Usually yes. Dedicated pages make it easier for Google and potential customers to understand exactly what you offer.
