A few things you might be wondering.

Is OfficeOps hidden surveillance?

No. Agents run visibly, monitoring is consent-first, and screen sharing is user-present and view-only.

Do you record microphone audio?

No. Only microphone state transitions and durations are tracked — never audio content.

Can I export reports?

Yes. CSV on Starter; CSV, Excel and PDF on Business and Enterprise.
