Project Context

One software capability, adapted to two different client identities.

Payroll tools handle sensitive business processes, so trust and clarity matter from the first interaction. A generic interface often weakens the sense of ownership the client needs around the product.

This rollout focused on delivering payroll access experiences that felt branded, recognizable, and better aligned to the companies using them.

What Changed
  • Two branded variants Separate visual treatments for Lehigh Business Solutions and Elite Debt Management Ltd.
  • Client-specific presentation The payroll experience was presented as part of each client's own environment rather than a neutral system.
  • Better proof of delivery These screens give prospects a tangible example of software customization beyond general marketing claims.
