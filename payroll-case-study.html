Project Context

One payroll system, adapted for two different client brands.

Payroll systems handle sensitive processes, so trust and clarity matter from the first screen. A generic interface can make the product feel less owned.

This rollout focused on making the payroll access experience feel more branded and more connected to each client.

What Changed
  • Two branded variants Separate visual treatments for Lehigh Business Solutions and Elite Debt Management Ltd.
  • Client-specific presentation The payroll experience felt like part of each client's own environment instead of a neutral system.
  • A clearer example of the work These screens give a real example of software customization instead of a vague claim.
