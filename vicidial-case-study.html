Project Context

A better software experience usually starts with adapting the system to the client.

VICIdial is a powerful system, but the default setup can feel generic. When the software is client-facing, the way it looks and feels matters too.

This rollout focused on making the dialer feel more client-owned through branded login treatment and a cleaner presentation.

What Changed
  • Branded entry point A login experience tied to Lehigh Business Solutions instead of a default system look.
  • Operational fit A setup shaped around real contact-center use instead of a one-size-fits-all install.
  • Proof of capability A real example of software customization built around a client's needs.
