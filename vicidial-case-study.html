Project Context

A stronger software experience starts with adapting the system to the client.

VICIdial is powerful, but default enterprise software often feels generic to end users. For a client-facing environment, brand fit and visual clarity matter because they influence trust, usability, and adoption.

This rollout focused on giving the dialer environment a more client-owned presence through branded login treatment and a cleaner presentation layer.

What Changed
  • Branded entry point A client-facing login experience tied to Lehigh Business Solutions rather than a generic software default.
  • Operational fit A deployment positioned around real contact-center use rather than a one-size-fits-all install.
  • Proof of capability A visible example of G&G Marketing handling software customization around practical business needs.
